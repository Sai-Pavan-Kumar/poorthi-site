Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no server to pay for, so there is no bill to pass on to you.

Can I install it today?

Not yet. {{NAME}} is being built now and will arrive on the Chrome Web Store.

How is this better than Chrome autofill?

Chrome handles names and addresses. {{NAME}} also handles notice period, CTC, experience ranges, your resume and the custom answers you save.

Is my data completely safe?

It never leaves your device, but it is not encrypted by default. Anyone with access to your computer profile could read it, so keep that profile to yourself.

Does it work without internet?

Filling needs no internet of its own, and saving your details works offline. The job site itself still has to load.

What if it fills something wrong?

Every filled field is highlighted, a review list shows what happened, and Undo restores the form as it was.
